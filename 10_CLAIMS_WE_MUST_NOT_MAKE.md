# Prohibited or unsupported claims

- “Veyra invented inventory integrity, reconciliation, anomaly/root-cause analysis, cycle counts or next-best verification.” Counterexamples [S1–S6].
- “Nobody else blocks picks based on stale physical evidence.” Public research was incomplete; vendors/custom ERP rules may already do this.
- “SAP/NetSuite/RGIS/Oruvo Stock Integrity lack the capability.” Their product details were not independently verifiable here.
- “Oruvo.ai proves Oruvo Stock Integrity exists / does not exist.” The homepage is unrelated to that specific named offering.
- “A score is a calibrated probability of physical stock being correct.” No calibration or reference set.
- “Our hypotheses prove root causes (theft, fraud, duplicate movement).” Ledger/count mismatch cannot establish cause.
- “An LLM predicts discrepancies or automatically reconciles any system.” No such implementation or evidence.
- “No movement can race a count or checkout.” In-memory prototype has no atomic DB commit or concurrent sessions.
- “The working Veyra dashboard/auth/multiwarehouse/substrate was verified.” No source files were provided.
- “11 passing synthetic tests prove customer savings, deployment readiness, hardware accuracy or regulatory audit compliance.” They do not.
- “The receipt is an error because it made the count stale.” Stale only means the old observation no longer attests to the current balance; a receipt can be legitimate.
